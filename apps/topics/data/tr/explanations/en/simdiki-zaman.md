Şimdiki Zaman, the present continuous formed with -yor, expresses actions currently happening or ongoing in the present period. This tense emphasizes actions in progress rather than general truths or permanent states.

## When to use the present continuous

Use -yor for actions occurring right now or in real time. You may also use this form for temporary habits in the present period or actions that are definitely going to happen very soon. The emphasis is on the action being in motion or currently true.

- Şu anda yağmur yağıyor. (It is raining right now.)
- Hergün sabah yüzüyor. (Every morning he swims.)
- Pazartesi annesini ziyaret ediyor. (On Monday he is visiting his mother.)

## Forming the positive

Take the verb root (the infinitive without -mek or -mak) and add -yor with vowel harmony. The vowel before -yor harmonizes with the last vowel of the root. If the root ends in a, ı, o, or u, use back vowels (ıyor, uyor). If the root ends in e, i, ö, or ü, use front vowels (iyor, üyor).

- Almak → alıyor (buying)
- Gelmek → geliyor (coming)
- Bulmak → buluyor (finding)
- Koşmak → koşuyor (running)
- Görüşmek → görüşüyor (meeting)
- Yemek → yiyor (eating)

## Forming the negative

Form the negative with -miyor, -muyor, -mıyor, or -müyor, following the same vowel harmony rules. The vowel after the -m- must match the harmony of the root's last vowel.

- Almamak → almıyor (not buying)
- Gelmemek → gelmiyor (not coming)
- Bulmamak → bulmuyor (not finding)
- Koşmamak → koşmuyor (not running)
- Görüşmemek → görüşmüyor (not meeting)
- Yememek → yemiyor (not eating)

## Forming the question

Add the question particle -mu, -mı, -mi, or -mü after the verb form. The particle's vowel harmonizes with the last vowel of the root. The question particle must come at the very end, even when a person suffix is present.

- Oynuyor mu? (Is he playing?)
- Çalışıyor musunuz? (Are you working?)
- Gelmiyor muyum? (Am I not coming?)
- Uyuyor muyuz? (Are we sleeping?)

## Vowel harmony and spelling rules

Vowel harmony is the most important rule for building -yor correctly. Identify the last vowel in the verb root. Back vowels (a, ı, o, u) require back vowels in the suffix; front vowels (e, i, ö, ü) require front vowels. This rule applies to the positive, negative, and question forms.

- Back vowels in root → -ıyor, -uyor, -mıyor, -muyor
- Front vowels in root → -iyor, -üyor, -miyor, -müyor

Remember that the harmony is determined by the last vowel you hear or see in the root, not by whether the root contains other vowels.

## Common time expressions with -yor

The present continuous often appears with specific time markers that emphasize the present moment or current period.

- Şu anda, şimdi (right now)
- Şu sırada, bu sırada (at this moment)
- Bugün (today)
- Bu hafta (this week)
- Bu ay (this month)
- Şu sıralar (these days)

Sorular: Şu anda ne yapıyorsun? Bugün ne giyiyorsun? Bu ay hangi kitabı okuyorsun?

## Common learner mistakes

The most frequent mistake is confusing -yor with the aorist (-er, -ar, -ir, -ur). The aorist describes general habits or permanent truths; -yor shows action in progress or temporary situations. Incorrect: Şu anda ders yapar. Correct: Şu anda ders yapıyorum.

Vowel harmony errors in the suffix are also common. Learners sometimes use the wrong vowel, especially with less familiar roots. Incorrect: Yazyor. Correct: Yazıyor.

Wrong question particle placement happens frequently. The question particle must come at the very end after any person suffix. Incorrect: Geliyor mi muyum? Correct: Geliyor muyum?

Another mistake is mixing the negative infinitive with the negative present continuous. These are not the same form. Incorrect: Gelmeme (confusing infinitive). Correct: Gelmiyor (negative present continuous).

Learners sometimes assume -yor means only immediate present. However, -yor can describe habitual present actions, repeated behaviors, or near-future certainties. The context determines the exact meaning.
