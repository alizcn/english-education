Le Present Perfect Continuous, aussi appelé Present Perfect Progressive, est un temps qui combine le present perfect et l'aspect continu. Il exprime une action qui a commencé dans le passé, qui continue au présent, ou qui vient juste de se terminer, en mettant l'accent sur la durée et le processus plutôt que sur le résultat final.

## Quand l'utiliser

- Pour exprimer une action qui a commencé dans le passé et qui continue jusqu'à maintenant: I have been waiting for two hours.
- Pour parler d'une action qui vient juste de s'arrêter et dont les conséquences sont visibles au présent: She has been crying. Her eyes are red.
- Pour montrer l'effort ou la durée investie dans une activité: He has been studying English for five years.
- Pour exprimer une situation temporaire mais en cours: We have been living in Paris since 2020.

## La formation du Present Perfect Continuous

Le Present Perfect Continuous se forme avec: have/has + been + verbe -ing

- have ou has selon le sujet
- have s'utilise avec: I, you, we, they
- has s'utilise avec: he, she, it
- been est le participe passé de be
- le verbe à la forme -ing (progressive)

Exemples: I have been playing tennis. / She has been working here since 2019. / They have been building a house.

## Les formes positive, négative et interrogative

Forme positive:
- I have been learning French.
- You have been watching television all afternoon.
- They have been working on this project.

Forme négative:
- Ajoutez not après have/has: have not been ou has not been
- Contractions courantes: haven't been, hasn't been
- I haven't been feeling well lately.
- She hasn't been sleeping properly.
- They haven't been answering their emails.

Forme interrogative:
- Inversez have/has et le sujet: Have/Has + sujet + been + verbe -ing?
- Have you been exercising regularly?
- Has he been practicing the piano?
- Have they been living here long?

## Les marqueurs temporels typiques

- for (depuis combien de temps): I have been playing guitar for ten years.
- since (depuis quand): We have been friends since high school.
- how long: How long have you been working there?
- all day / all week / all month: It has been raining all day.
- recently: I have been feeling tired recently.
- lately: Have you been hearing the news lately?

## Les erreurs courantes des apprenants

- Oublier been: I have been studying et non I have studying ou I am studying since 2020
- Confondre avec le present perfect simple: I have lived here for 5 years (focus sur l'existence) vs I have been living here for 5 years (focus sur la durée)
- Utiliser le present simple avec for/since: I am learning English for 2 years est incorrect; utilisez I have been learning English for 2 years
- Oublier la forme -ing du verbe: She has been walk au lieu de She has been walking
- Mal utiliser haven't/hasn't: I not have been au lieu de I haven't been

## Différences avec le Present Perfect Simple

Present Perfect Simple exprime le résultat: I have finished my homework. (c'est fait)

Present Perfect Continuous exprime la durée: I have been doing my homework. (je suis en train depuis un moment)

Comparaisons:
- He has worked here for 5 years (résultat: il a l'expérience)
- He has been working here for 5 years (durée: il est toujours en train)

Certains verbes n'aiment pas la forme continue (verbes d'état): know, understand, love, want. I have known her for 10 years (non I have been knowing her)
