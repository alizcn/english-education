Le Past Perfect, ou plus-que-parfait anglais, est un temps verbal qui exprime une action passée antérieure à une autre action passée. C'est le temps du « avant le passé », utilisé pour montrer la relation chronologique entre deux événements au passé.

## Quand utiliser le Past Perfect

- Pour montrer qu'une action s'est déroulée avant une autre action au passé: I had already eaten when they arrived.
- Pour expliquer une situation passée qui résulte d'une action antérieure: The project failed because we hadn't planned it properly.
- Pour montrer la cause d'une émotion ou d'une réaction au passé: She felt happy because she had passed her exam.
- Pour raconter une histoire avec clarté chronologique: When I opened the door, I realized they had gone.

## Comment former le Past Perfect

Forme positive: Sujet + had + participe passé. Exemple: They had finished the work before noon. Autre exemple: She had studied for three hours.

Forme négative: Sujet + had + not + participe passé, ou la contraction hadn't. Exemple: We hadn't met before that day. Exemple: He hadn't seen that movie.

Forme interrogative: Had + sujet + participe passé. Exemple: Had you finished your homework? Autre exemple: Had they already left when you called?

## Le participe passé

- Verbes réguliers: ajouter -ed au radical. Exemple: walk → walked, talk → talked, play → played.
- Verbes irréguliers: formes spéciales à mémoriser. go → gone, eat → eaten, see → seen, write → written, break → broken, buy → bought, bring → brought.
- Attention: le verbe « have » devient toujours « had » au passé, jamais « hadded ».
- Les verbes en -y deviennent -ied: study → studied, cry → cried.

## Les expressions temporelles courantes

- Before: I had finished before he arrived.
- After: After she had left, they started the meeting.
- Already: He had already called when I woke up.
- When: When I arrived, they had already started.
- By the time: By the time we got there, they had finished.
- Never: I had never seen anything like that.
- Just: She had just arrived when the phone rang.

## Les erreurs courantes

Erreur 1 - Confondre Past Perfect et Simple Past: Incorrect: When I arrived, they ate dinner. Correct: When I arrived, they had eaten dinner. (Ils avaient mangé avant mon arrivée.)

Erreur 2 - Utiliser Past Perfect pour des événements simultanés au passé: Incorrect: I went to the cinema and I had watched a film. Correct: I went to the cinema and watched a film.

Erreur 3 - Oublier « had » avant le participe passé: Incorrect: She eaten the apple when I came. Correct: She had eaten the apple when I came.

Erreur 4 - Utiliser la base form au lieu du participe passé après « had »: Incorrect: They had go home. Correct: They had gone home.

Erreur 5 - Mauvaise contraction à la forme interrogative: Incorrect: Had she finish the work? Correct: Had she finished the work?
