Das Past Continuous, auch "Past Progressive" genannt, beschreibt eine Handlung, die in der Vergangenheit stattgefunden hat und zu einem bestimmten Moment noch nicht abgeschlossen war. Diese Form wird sehr häufig verwendet, um die zeitliche Beziehung zwischen zwei Ereignissen in der Vergangenheit auszudrücken.

## Verwendung des Past Continuous

- Handlung, die in der Vergangenheit im Gange war, als etwas anderes passierte: I was cooking dinner when my friend called.
- Zwei gleichzeitige Handlungen in der Vergangenheit: While I was studying, my sister was watching television.
- Eine Hintergrund-Handlung für eine andere Aktion: It was raining when we arrived at the station.
- Wiederholte oder kontinuierliche Handlungen über einen längeren Zeitraum: They were building a new house all summer.

## Die positive Form

- Struktur: was/were + Verb + -ing
- Personenformen: I was, you were, he/she/it was, we were, they were
- Beispiele: I was reading a book. She was working on the project. They were preparing for the exam.
- Das Grundverb ändert sich nicht, nur die Form von "be" wird konjugiert.

## Negative und Fragen

- Negative Form: was/were + not + Verb + -ing
- Beispiele Negativ: I was not eating. She wasn't sleeping. They weren't listening to the music.
- Fragen: Was/Were + Person + Verb + -ing?
- Beispiele Fragen: Were you watching the game? Was he doing his homework? Were they enjoying the party?
- In Fragen steht was oder were am Anfang, danach folgt das Subjekt und dann das Verb mit -ing.

## Bildung von -ing: Regeln für Rechtschreibung

- Einfach anhängen: walk → walking, study → studying, watch → watching
- Auslautendes "e" wird entfernt: make → making, write → writing, take → taking
- Auslautender Konsonant nach kurzem Vokal wird verdoppelt: run → running, stop → stopping, sit → sitting
- Aber: Kein Verdoppeln nach Diphthongen oder Langvokalen: read → reading, float → floating

## Zeitausdrücke und Signalwörter

- While: While I was working, the power went out.
- When: When we arrived, they were having dinner.
- As: As they were leaving, I saw them.
- All day, all night, the whole time: He was sleeping all morning.
- At that moment, at that time: At 3 o'clock yesterday, I was at the gym.
- During: During the meeting, I was taking notes.

## Häufige Fehler von Deutschsprachigen

- Fehler: "I was go to the shop." - Richtig: I was going to the shop. (Viele vergessen das -ing)
- Fehler: "She was not work." - Richtig: She was not working. (Wieder das -ing vergessen)
- Fehler: "Was you writing?" - Richtig: Were you writing? (Falsche Person von "be")
- Fehler: "While I was sleeping, someone has stolen my wallet." - Richtig: While I was sleeping, someone stole my wallet. (Simple Past, nicht Present Perfect)
- Fehler: "I was understanding the lesson." - Richtig: I understood the lesson. (Stative Verben wie understand, know, have, want verwenden normalerweise kein Continuous)

## Past Continuous vs. Simple Past

- Simple Past beschreibt abgeschlossene Handlungen; Past Continuous beschreibt, was noch im Gange war: He finished his work. vs. He was finishing his work when I arrived.
- "Während" (while) mit längerer Handlung nimmt Past Continuous; die unterbrochene Handlung steht im Simple Past: While I was watching TV, the phone rang.
- Zwei kurze, aufeinanderfolgende Handlungen stehen im Simple Past: I woke up, got dressed, and went to work.
- Zwei gleichzeitige, längere Handlungen nutzen beide Past Continuous: While they were dancing, we were talking.
