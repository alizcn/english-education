The passive voice is a way of forming a sentence where we focus on the action or the person or thing that receives the action, rather than the person or thing that performs the action. In passive sentences, the object of the active sentence becomes the subject, and the verb form changes to show this new structure.

## When and Why We Use the Passive Voice

- We use the passive voice when we want to emphasize what happened rather than who did it: The bridge was built in 1995.
- We use it when we do not know who performed the action: My wallet was stolen.
- We use it when the action is more important than the agent: The letter was delivered this morning.
- We use it in formal writing, especially in scientific or academic contexts: The experiment was conducted over three months.
- We use it when it is obvious who performed the action or when it is not necessary to mention the agent: The president was elected last week.

## How to Build the Passive Voice in Affirmative Statements

The passive voice is built using the verb to be plus the past participle of the main verb.

- The basic structure is: subject + appropriate form of be + past participle.
- The tense of the sentence is shown by the form of be, not by changes to the main verb.
- Example in present simple: The report is written by the manager.
- Example in past simple: The report was written by the manager.
- Example in present continuous: The report is being written by the manager.
- Example in past continuous: The report was being written by the manager.
- Example in present perfect: The report has been written by the manager.
- Example in past perfect: The report had been written by the manager.
- Example in future simple: The report will be written by the manager.

## Building Negative Forms

To make a negative passive sentence, add not after the form of be.

- Present simple negative: The report is not written by the manager.
- Past simple negative: The report was not written by the manager.
- Present continuous negative: The report is not being written by the manager.
- Present perfect negative: The report has not been written by the manager.
- Future simple negative: The report will not be written by the manager.
- The word not comes immediately after the auxiliary verb: Coffee is not served after 9 pm.
- In contracted form: The invitations weren't sent in time. (was not becomes wasn't; were not becomes weren't)

## Building Questions in the Passive Voice

In passive questions, the form of be comes before the subject.

- Structure: auxiliary verb + subject + past participle?
- Present simple question: Is the report written by the manager?
- Past simple question: Was the report written by the manager?
- Present continuous question: Is the report being written by the manager?
- Present perfect question: Has the report been written by the manager?
- Future simple question: Will the report be written by the manager?
- With question words: Who was the letter written by? What was discovered during the investigation? When will the building be completed?

## Passive Voice with Different Tenses

The past participle of the main verb never changes in passive structures. Only the form of be changes to show different tenses.

- Present simple: The documents are reviewed every month.
- Past simple: The documents were reviewed last month.
- Present continuous: The documents are being reviewed right now.
- Past continuous: The documents were being reviewed when I arrived.
- Present perfect: The documents have been reviewed for accuracy.
- Past perfect: The documents had been reviewed before the meeting.
- Future simple: The documents will be reviewed next week.
- Future continuous: The documents will be being reviewed all next week.
- Future perfect: The documents will have been reviewed by Friday.
- Modal verbs: The documents should be reviewed carefully. The documents could have been damaged during shipping.

## Common Mistakes Learners Make

Many learners make predictable errors with the passive voice.

- Mistake: Using the base verb instead of the past participle. Wrong: The house is build. Correct: The house is built.
- Mistake: Forgetting to use a form of be. Wrong: The letter sent yesterday. Correct: The letter was sent yesterday.
- Mistake: Using the wrong form of be for the tense. Wrong: The report is wrote by the manager. Correct: The report is written by the manager.
- Mistake: Putting by in the wrong place. Wrong: The book was written by Stephen King author. Correct: The book was written by Stephen King.
- Mistake: Using the passive voice when the active voice is clearer. Wrong: The decision was made by us. Better: We made the decision.
- Mistake: Overusing the passive voice in informal conversation. The passive voice is formal, so use it mainly in writing and formal speech.
- Mistake: Forgetting to include be in negative sentences. Wrong: The files not sent yet. Correct: The files have not been sent yet.

## The Agent: When to Include by

The agent is the person or thing that performs the action. You do not always need to include the agent in a passive sentence.

- Use by when it is important to mention who or what performed the action: The novel was written by Jane Austen.
- Omit the agent when it is unknown: The car was stolen.
- Omit the agent when it is obvious: The president was elected.
- Omit the agent when the focus is on the action itself: The work was completed on time.
- In formal and academic writing, the agent is often omitted: The experiment showed interesting results.
