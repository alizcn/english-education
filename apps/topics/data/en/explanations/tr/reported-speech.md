Reported speech (indirect speech), birinin söylediği sözleri doğrudan tırnak içinde değil, başka birinin sözüyle aktarmakta kullanılır. "He said that he was tired" gibi cümleler, orijinal sözü ("I am tired") raporlamak için yapılır. Bu yapı, iki kişi arasındaki konuşmaları anlatırken veya geçmiş olayları bildirirken önemlidir.

## Ne zaman kullanılır?

- Birinin söylediği sözü başka birine aktarırken: I told her that I would help her.
- Yazılı veya sözlü anlatılarda geçmiş konuşmaları bildirirken: She said that the meeting was postponed.
- İlmi yazılarda veya raporlarda: The research team concluded that the results were significant.
- Tırnak işaretleri olmadan, daha resmi veya dokümanter bir üslupla anlatmak istediğinizde.

## Temel yapı ve başlangıç fiilleri

- En sık kullanılan yapı: say, tell, inform, ask, answer, explain, announce, report, mention, declare şeklindedir.
- "Say" ve "tell" arasında fark vardır: "tell" direktnesi alır (tell someone), "say" almayabilir: She said that she was late. But: She told me that she was late.
- Yapı: Reporting verb + that + reported clause: He said that he was leaving.
- "That" çoğunlukla yazılı İngilizcede kalabilir: He said he was leaving.

## Olumlu cümleler

- Direct speech: "I am happy." → Reported speech: She said that she was happy.
- Direct speech: "I have finished my work." → Reported speech: He told me that he had finished his work.
- Zaman değişimi kuralları: present tense → past tense, past tense → past perfect, future → conditional (would/should).
- Zamirler de değişir: I → he/she, you → I/he/she, my → his/her: "I love my job" becomes "He said that he loved his job."
- Yer ve zaman belirteçleri değişir: "here" becomes "there", "now" becomes "then", "today" becomes "that day": She said that she was happy then.

## Olumsuz cümleler

- Direct speech: "I am not ready." → Reported speech: He said that he was not ready.
- Olumsuzluk "not" hali değişmez, ancak önceki fiil zamanı değişir: She said she hadn't seen him.
- "Don't/doesn't/didn't" yapıları reported speech'te "not" haliyle verilir: "Don't go" becomes He told me not to go. (infinitive kullanıldığında)
- Yan cümlede "not" konumu: Subject + verb + not, değil subject + not + verb: She said that she did not like coffee (not: She said that she not liked coffee).

## Sorular ve komutlar

- Sorular "if/whether" ile başlar: "Do you like pizza?" becomes "He asked if I liked pizza."
- Soru sözcükleri korunur: "Where do you live?" becomes "She asked where I lived."
- Soru cümlesinde fiil sırası değişir (inversion kaldırılır): "Can you help me?" becomes "He asked if I could help him."
- Komutlar "to + infinitive" yapısı alır: "Close the door!" becomes "She told me to close the door."
- Olumsuz komutlar: "Don't be late" becomes "She told him not to be late."

## Zaman değişiklikleri (Tense Shift)

- Present simple → past simple: "I work here" becomes "She said that she worked there."
- Present continuous → past continuous: "I am studying" becomes "He said that he was studying."
- Present perfect → past perfect: "I have eaten" becomes "She said that she had eaten."
- Past tense → past perfect: "I went to Paris" becomes "He said that he had gone to Paris."
- Future (will) → would: "I will come" becomes "She said that she would come."
- Modals da değişir: "I can do it" becomes "He said that he could do it."; "I may visit you" becomes "She said that she might visit me."
- Eğer reported clause geçmişte anlatılıyorsa ve hala geçerli ise tense değişmeyebilir: "The teacher said that Paris is the capital of France." (gerçek, değişmez)

## Yaygın hatalar

- Zaman değişimini unutmak: "He said that he is tired" (yanlış) → "He said that he was tired" (doğru).
- "Say" ve "tell" karıştırmak: "He said to me that..." (yanlış, "say" doğrudan nesne almaz) → "He told me that..." (doğru).
- Zamirler değiştirilmemesi: "She said that I am happy" (yanlış, karışık) → "She said that she was happy" (doğru).
- Soru cümlelerinde "that" kullanmak: "He asked that where do I live" (yanlış) → "He asked where I lived" (doğru).
- Question word sorularında "if" kullanmak: "She asked if where he lived" (yanlış) → "She asked where he lived" (doğru).
- Reported speech'te inversion kaldırılmadığında yapılan hata: "He asked did I understand" (yanlış) → "He asked if I understood" (doğru).
- Yer ve zaman belirteçlerini değiştirilmemesi: "He said that I will see you here tomorrow" (yanlış) → "He said that he would see me there the next day" (doğru).
