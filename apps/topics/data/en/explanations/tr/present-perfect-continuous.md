Present Perfect Continuous, bir eylem veya durumun belirli bir zaman önce başladığını, şu anda hâlâ devam ettiğini veya yakın zamanda bittiğini belirtir. Bu yapı, eylemin süresi ve sonuçları önemli olduğunda kullanılır ve "have/has been" ile "verb + -ing" kombinasyonundan oluşur.

## Kullanım Alanları

Su anda devam eden eylem: I have been studying English for three years. She has been working at this company since 2020.

Yakın zamanda biten eylem: I have been running, so I am tired now. He has been cooking all morning.

Eylemin ne kadar süredir devam ettiğini vurgulamak: They have been playing football for two hours. We have been waiting for the bus since 10 AM.

Bir durumun etkilerini şu anda görmek: The children have been playing outside, and they are very dirty now. She has been crying, and her eyes are red.

## Yapısı: Olumlu Cümleler

Present Perfect Continuous temel yapısı: have/has + been + verb + -ing

- I have been working
- You have been working
- He/She/It has been working
- We have been working
- You have been working
- They have been working

Örnekler: I have been playing tennis since morning. She has been learning French for six months. They have been discussing the project all day. My brother has been saving money for a new car.

## Olumsuz ve Soru Cümleleri

Olumsuz cümlelerde "not" kelimesi "have" veya "has" sonrasına gelir: I have not been sleeping well lately. She has not been attending classes. They have not been practicing their instruments.

Soru cümleleri "Have/Has" ile başlar: Have you been working here long? Has he been studying hard? Have they been waiting long?

Olumsuz soru cümleleri: Haven't you been feeling well? Hasn't she been sleeping enough? Haven't they been practicing?

## Zaman İfadeleri ve Belirteçleri

Present Perfect Continuous belirli zaman ifadeleriyle sıkça birlikte kullanılır:

- for + zaman süresi: for two hours, for five years, for ages
- since + başlangıç zamanı: since morning, since 2020, since Monday, since childhood
- all day, all week, all month, all afternoon
- recently, lately, just, over the past few days

Örnekler: I have been waiting for an hour. She has been living here since childhood. We have been talking about this all afternoon. He has been improving his skills recently. They have been arguing over this issue for weeks.

## Present Perfect ile Fark

Present Perfect Continuous ve Present Perfect arasında önemli bir fark vardır. Present Perfect eylemin tamamlandığını vurgularken, Present Perfect Continuous devam eden veya yakın zamanda tamamlanan eylemi vurgular:

- I have studied English. (çalışmayı bitirdim, ne zaman bittiği önemsiz)
- I have been studying English. (hâlâ çalışıyorum, süresi önemli)

Sayılabilir ve kaç kez yapıldığı önemliyse Present Perfect kullanırız: I have written five emails. He has visited Paris three times.

Eylemin süresi veya hâlâ devam edip etmediği önemliyse Present Perfect Continuous kullanırız: I have been writing emails since this morning. She has been working on this project for two weeks.

## Yaygın Hatalar

Türkçe konuşanlar bu şekillerde sıkça hata yaparlar:

Hata: I am studying English for three years. Doğru: I have been studying English for three years.

Hata: She has been working since 10 years. Doğru: She has been working for 10 years. Since ile gün, saat, tarih; for ile zaman süresi kullanılır.

Hata: Have you been going to school? Doğru: Do you go to school? Düzenli, tekrarlanan eylemler için Present Simple veya "Do you go" daha doğru olabilir.

Hata: I have been coding a program. Doğru: I have coded a program. Tamamlanan, sayılabilir eylemler için Present Perfect daha uygun olabilir.

Hata: He has been knowing English. Doğru: He has known English. State verbs (bilmek, istemek, sevmek, görmek, duymak) genellikle Present Perfect Continuous ile kullanılmaz.

## Yazım Kuralları: -ing Eki

-ing yapısı eklenirken yazım kurallarına dikkat edilir:

Sessiz harfle biten tek heceli fiillerde harfi iki katına çıkarabilirsiniz: run → running, stop → stopping, sit → sitting, swim → swimming

E ile biten fiillerde e harfi çıkarılır: make → making, take → taking, live → living, move → moving

IE ile biten fiillerde bu harfler y'ye dönüşür: die → dying, lie → lying

Y ile biten fiillerde y'nin yerine ying gelir: study → studying, try → trying, carry → carrying
