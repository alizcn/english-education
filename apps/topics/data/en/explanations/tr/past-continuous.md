Past Continuous (Geçmiş Zaman Devam Hali), belirli bir zamanda geçmişte devam etmekte olan bir eylemi anlatır. İngilizcede çok sık kullanılan bir zaman yapısıdır ve özellikle iki eylemin aynı anda gerçekleştiğini göstermek için önemlidir.

## Tanım ve Kullanım

Past Continuous, geçmişte belirli bir anda veya belirli bir zaman diliminde devam etmekte olan eylemleri ifade eder. Bu zaman yapısını şu durumlarda kullanırız:

- Geçmişte belirli bir anda devam eden eylem: I was reading a book when you called.
- Geçmişte aynı anda iki eylemin olması: While she was cooking dinner, I was setting the table.
- Kesintiye uğrayan eylemler: He was watching TV when the power went out.
- Eylemlerinin geçmişteki süresi hakkında: They were living in Paris for five years.

## Yapı

Past Continuous (was/were + present participle) şeklinde oluşturulur. Özne ve "was/were" kombinasyonuna dikkat etmek önemlidir.

Olumlu Cümleler: Özne + was/were + verb+ing. I was sleeping. She was studying. They were dancing. We were waiting.

Olumsuz Cümleler: Özne + was/were + not + verb+ing. I was not sleeping. She was not studying. They were not dancing. We were not waiting.

Soru Cümleler: Was/were + özne + verb+ing? Was I sleeping? Were you studying? Were they dancing? Was she waiting?

## Zaman Belirteçleri

Past Continuous genellikle belirli zaman belirteçleriyle kullanılır. Bunlar eylemin geçmişteki hangi anda devam ettiğini gösterir.

- At this time yesterday: I was working at this time yesterday.
- At 3 o'clock: He was sleeping at 3 o'clock in the morning.
- While: While you were sleeping, I made breakfast.
- When: When he arrived, I was preparing the dinner.
- All day: She was studying all day.
- Yesterday afternoon: We were playing football yesterday afternoon.
- At that moment: They were arguing at that moment.

## Yazım Kuralları

Verb+ing şekli oluştururken bazı yazım kurallarına uyulması gerekir.

- Çoğu fiil için sadece -ing eklenir: play - playing, run - running, sleep - sleeping.
- Sonu e ile biten fiiller için e silinir ve -ing eklenir: write - writing, make - making, take - taking.
- Tek heceli ve son harfi tek soru yazılan fiiller için son harf iki kez yazılır: sit - sitting, stop - stopping, swim - swimming.
- Sonu -ie olan fiiller -ie silinir -y yazılır: lie - lying, die - dying.

## Sık Yapılan Hatalar

Öğrenciler Past Continuous kullanırken bazı tipik hatalar yaparlar.

- Hata: I was go to the cinema. / Doğru: I was going to the cinema.
- Hata: While I studied, he was sleeping. / Doğru: While I was studying, he was sleeping.
- Hata: Was she sleeping at 10 o'clock yesterday? No, she was not. / Yanlış değil ama daha doğal: No, she wasn't.
- Hata: They was playing football. / Doğru: They were playing football. (was sadece I ile, were diğer hepsiyle)
- Hata: I was study the lesson. / Doğru: I was studying the lesson. (verb+ing kullanmalı, temel fiil değil)
- Hata: When you called me, I sleepinged. / Doğru: When you called me, I was sleeping. (ing yazımı sadece bir kez)

## Past Continuous vs Simple Past

Past Continuous ile Simple Past arasındaki fark önemlidir. İki yapı çoğu zaman birlikte kullanılır.

- Simple Past tam olmuş eylemler için kullanılır: She finished her work.
- Past Continuous devam eden eylemler için kullanılır: She was finishing her work.
- İki eylemle: While I was reading, the phone rang. (reading devam ediyor, ring tamamlanıyor)
- Hata: When he was arriving, I left. / Doğru: When he arrived, I was leaving.
